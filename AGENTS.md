Use `--full` after validator/cache changes or to diagnose a mismatch, then compare with the cached result. Run proportionate regression tests; do not rerun passing checks without changed inputs or a concrete concern. - Update the small current state and STATUS at meaningful boundaries. Keep detailed evidence once in its own record; search archived history with `project_work history`. Do not append another changelog paragraph to README or duplicate evidence into every inherited review.
