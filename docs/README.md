## Versions and historical records

The language edition is **1.0**. Native syntax versions **0.14–0.16** and the Python package version identify different things; they are not competing completion statuses. The [release manifest](../data/language-release-v1.json) selects the fixed specifications and evidence.

The versioned Japanese documents in this directory include original specifications, semantic reviews, and development reports. They remain at their original paths because evidence refers to their exact contents. Their titles may say “prototype” and their final sections may describe work that was subsequently completed. Use the current guides for an introduction and the release manifest for the selected baseline.

The [original release record](language-edition-v1.md) and [original circle specification](circle-writing-v2.md) are Japanese archival sources. The guides explain their current user-facing content in both languages; they do not rewrite historical judgments or translate every archived review. Dictionary meaning records also retain their original Japanese wording; see [vocabulary](guide/vocabulary.md).

Maintainer-specific instructions remain in [WORKFLOW](WORKFLOW.md), [project records](project-records.md), and [long jobs](long-jobs.md), in Japanese. They describe the private handoff database and evidence-maintenance process, which are not prerequisites for reading or parsing Noema.
